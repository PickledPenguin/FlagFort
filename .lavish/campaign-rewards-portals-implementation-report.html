BEHAVIOR CHANGE

Locked means locked

BEFORE

Level alone released rewards

  • Coin milestones beyond locked tiers could settle globally.
  • Tier cards only highlighted and relied on a footer summary.
  • Every bounty carried an artificial Night 5 completion gate.
  • Portal relocation could resolve near its destroyed position.
IMPLEMENTED

Progression and state are explicit

  • Each milestone checks both level and owning-tier unlock state.
  • Selected tier cards flip to three threats, boss mechanics, requirements, and play controls.
  • Bounties complete immediately when their target is met.
  • Relocatable portals must move at least 580 world units.
