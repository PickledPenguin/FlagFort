SYSTEM BEHAVIOR

Forest stays familiar, Snowbound changes the run

Forest Frontier

  • Base game visuals and existing roster selection remain intact.
  • Available at Level 1 without a prerequisite.
  • The original boss remains the tier boss.
  • Level 2 and 3 coin milestones reward early progress.

Snowbound Keep

  • Requires Level 4 and at least one Forest Frontier victory.
  • Guarantees Frostbite on Night 3, Snowballer on Night 5, and Icebound Crusher on Night 7.
  • Uses Frost Warden as the Night 10 boss.
  • Night snowfall fades in, then fades out at dawn. Resource caps are assigned by a stable tier-specific seed.

Reward safety

  • Rewards use a discriminated union ready for coins, cosmetics, and gameplay benefits.
  • Claimed milestone IDs persist and prevent duplicate grants.
  • Unlock detection compares pre-settlement and post-settlement profile state.

HUD refinement

  • END DAY now sits directly below the countdown clock.
  • The build bar is centered and visually independent.
  • Both 1280×720 and 960×540 browser checks passed after compact-layout adjustments.
