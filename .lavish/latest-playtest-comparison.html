Flag Fort playtest follow-up - 2026-08-04 - normal campaign

Endless remains unproven. Campaign preservation failed this run.

The newest live test never reached endless mode. A stronger, better-equipped level 22 profile built a larger and higher-tier fort, but lost when night 10 drove the flag from 25.8 prior damage to effectively zero health. The fort itself held better than before, so the regression is concentrated assault pacing and damage routing, not general structural fragility.

Recommendation: separate campaign pacing from the new endless pressure package before judging endless balance.

Causal diagnosis

Three mechanics stack at the campaign climax

The telemetry and implementation report line up on a concentrated pacing problem. Confidence is high on the mechanism, moderate on final tuning values because the seed and build differ.

Adaptive pressure responds to a stronger fort

1.848x

Night 10 adaptive multiplier, versus 1.374x in the prior campaign. Fort score was 3,362 against 2,081 expected, plus level and upgrade contributions.

The horde is compressed into the opening

15 sec

The implementation guarantees full schedule deployment by this cutoff. On night 10 that means 83 scheduled enemies, plus a boss, five summons, and seven children.

Damage reaches inner loss conditions

124.2

Night 10 flag damage: 75.9 from the boss and 48.3 from basics. The player also took 100.6 while only five structures were destroyed.

Important distinction: the new power-awareness term contributed only +0.126 to the night 10 multiplier. Most of the pressure came from the fort structure delta (+0.407), player level (+0.315), and mutation budget. The likely regression is not one isolated formula. It is the interaction between high campaign adaptation, a much larger schedule, and globally compressed spawn deployment.

Recommended next tuning pass

Protect the campaign, preserve the endless design

The best correction is mode-aware pacing. Do not weaken the whole horde or undo the endless architecture based on a campaign-only test.

P0 - campaign pacing

Scope the 15-second deployment cutoff to endless

Distribute campaign schedules across the normal fight window, especially on night 10. Keep the fast guaranteed deployment for endless where portal throughput was the original failure.

P0 - campaign adaptation

Add a campaign-specific pressure ceiling

Target roughly 55-70 scheduled enemies on night 10 for a strong normal build, then tune from repeated seeds. Avoid allowing structure investment to turn the campaign boss wave into an 83-enemy rush.

P0 - boss concurrency

Limit the overlap, not the boss identity

Retain the delayed boss and full-clear concept, but prevent the boss, most scheduled enemies, and summons from arriving in the same narrow window during campaign night 10.

P1 - validation

Replay two campaign seeds before endless

Use the latest seed i3xtc3-8h0mah and prior seed 3rbnwj-zyqvcn. Require normal victories with meaningful damage, a living threat to the flag, and a readable boss window.

P1 - endless evidence

Then run the intended 11-25 test

Measure zero-damage nights, special threat share, boss time-to-kill, flag-radius entries, turret damage share, and resource spending. The latest log says nothing about these post-campaign targets.

P1 - telemetry

Add spawn-time concentration signals

Record scheduled spawns by 5-second bucket and peak simultaneous population. That will make deployment compression directly measurable instead of inferred from configuration.
