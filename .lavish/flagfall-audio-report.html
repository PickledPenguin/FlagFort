Selection and processing

Source search

/Users/aiagents/Documents/GMTK/Audio was searched recursively. Kenney Impact, Interface, RPG, and UI SFX packs plus all five Sonniss Part 9 sections were accessible.

Rights boundary

Kenney assets use CC0 1.0. Sonniss Part 9 assets use the included royalty-free license. The unlabeled Audio/zombies folder was rejected in full because provenance and rights were unclear.

Conversion

Non-looping effects were trimmed below -55 dB, normalized with fixed gain, peak-capped below -1.25 dBFS, resampled to 48 kHz stereo, and encoded as OGG Opus at 80 kbps VBR. Ambience uses 64 kbps.

Source preservation

The pipeline stores each original SHA-256 hash and verifies it again after conversion. Source-folder files were never modified, moved, renamed, or deleted.

Important rejected candidates

Unlicensed zombie files: rejected due to missing creator, pack, source, and license data.

Boss acid liquid impact: rejected because it read as several impacts. A clean 0.52-second isolated spray was selected instead.

Long multi-variant and alarm files: rejected because unrelated material, 30 to 60-second length, or spoken wording would obscure combat and countdown timing.
